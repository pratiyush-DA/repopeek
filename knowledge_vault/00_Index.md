## Visual Graph Structure
The vault is designed with dense `[[wikilinks]]` and categorized `#tags` to render an interconnected knowledge graph in Obsidian:
- Nodes represent structural concepts, architectural decisions, schemas, and tasks.
- Edges represent conceptual dependencies, implementations, and derivations.
- Structural metadata and schemas are strictly formatted as JSON.
